# AI-Based Smart Study Planner

## Problem Statement
Students often face difficulty in managing their study time effectively, especially during exams. They struggle to create proper study schedules, prioritize difficult subjects, and maintain consistency. This project uses Artificial Intelligence to generate personalized study plans based on exam dates, subject difficulty, and available study time.

## Objective
- To help students prepare effectively for exams.
- To generate AI-based personalized study timetables.
- To reduce exam stress through proper planning.
- To improve time management and learning efficiency.

## Key Features
- AI-generated study timetable.
- Subject priority management.
- Exam countdown tracking.
- Daily study task recommendations.
- Progress monitoring.
- User-friendly interface.
